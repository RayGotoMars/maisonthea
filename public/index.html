Folds in three moves三步折好

  1. Fold in half对折

    Bring the brim together, front to back.把帽檐前后对折。

  2. Roll or fold again再卷或再折

    Roll from one side toward the other into a small bundle.从一边卷到另一边，变成小小一团。

  3. Pack it放进包里

    Unfold when you arrive. The brim springs back.到了再打开，帽檐自动恢复。

Théa

The name名字的由来

Light, in every sense.轻盈，也是光。

In Greek myth, Theia is the Titan of light, the mother of the sun, and the one who gives gold its shine. We named the house after her, because every hat here is made for a life lived in the sun.在希腊神话里，Theia 是光之女神、太阳的母亲，也是让黄金闪耀的神。我们用她的名字命名，因为这里的每一顶帽子，都为阳光下的生活而做。
