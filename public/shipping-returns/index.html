Shipping & returns运费与退换

Simple, and fair.简单，也公平。

Shipping运费

  • We currently ship within the United States.目前只寄送美国境内。
  • Free shipping on orders over $50.订单满 $50 包邮。
  • Under that, a flat rate is shown at checkout.不满时，结账页面会显示固定运费。
  • Every order gets a tracking number by email when it ships.发货后会通过邮件发送物流单号。

Returns退货

  • Return unworn hats in their original packaging within 30 days of delivery.收货后 30 天内，未佩戴、包装完好的帽子可以退货。
  • Return shipping is paid by the customer.退货运费由顾客承担。
  • Refunds go back to your original payment method once we receive the hat.收到退回的帽子后，退款会原路返回。

Exchanges换货

  • Your first color exchange is free.第一次换颜色免费。

If something's wrong如果收到的商品有问题

If your hat arrives damaged or with a defect, email us a photo. We'll send a replacement or a refund, and you won't need to send it back.如果收到的帽子有损坏或瑕疵，请拍照发邮件给我们。我们会补发新的或退款，不需要寄回。

Custom orders定制款

Custom and personalized pieces are made to order and are final sale.定制款按单制作，不退不换。

To start a return or exchange, email需要退换货，请发邮件到 hello@maisonthea.com with your order number.并附上订单号。

First to know about new colors.新颜色上市，第一时间通知你。
