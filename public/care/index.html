Caring for your hat如何保养

  1. Fold, don't crush折叠，别硬压

    Fold in half and roll gently. Keep heavy things off it in your bag.对折后轻轻卷起。放在包里时别压重物。

  2. Spot clean局部清洁

    Wipe marks with a damp cloth. No machine-washing or soaking.有污渍就用湿布擦。不要机洗或泡水。

  3. Air-dry, store open晾干，展开收纳

    Dry in the shade. At home, store it open so the brim rests.放在阴凉处晾干。在家时展开放，让帽檐休息。

Questions常见问题

How do I find my size?怎么选尺码？

You don't need to. Every hat is one size, with a hook-and-loop closure at the back that adjusts to fit.不用选。每一款都是均码，后侧魔术贴可以调节大小。

Will it really spring back after folding?折过之后真的能恢复原样吗？

Yes. Fold it in half, roll it, pack it. When you take it out, give the brim a gentle shake and it returns to shape.可以。对折、卷起、放进包里。拿出来以后轻轻抖一抖帽檐，就会恢复原样。

Can I wear a ponytail?可以扎马尾吗？

With the open-crown styles (The Riviera and The Malibu), yes. The adjustable back works with a low ponytail on The Signature, too.空顶款（里维埃拉、马里布）完全可以。经典款后侧可调节，低马尾也能戴。

How do I clean it?怎么清洁？

Spot clean with a damp cloth and let it air-dry away from direct heat. Please don't machine-wash, soak or tumble-dry.用湿布轻轻擦拭，放在阴凉处自然晾干。请不要机洗、泡水或烘干。

Where do you ship?可以寄到哪里？

Within the United States for now. Shipping is free on orders over $50.目前只发美国境内。满 $50 包邮。

Can I return or exchange?可以退换吗？

Yes, unworn hats within 30 days. Your first color exchange is free. Custom pieces are final sale.可以。未佩戴的帽子 30 天内可退换，第一次换颜色免费。定制款不退不换。

Do you offer wholesale?可以批发吗？

Yes. Boutiques, resort and airport shops, wedding planners and stylists are welcome to get in touch.可以。欢迎精品店、度假区和机场商店、婚礼策划和造型师联系我们。
