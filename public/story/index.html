Our story品牌故事

Made for a life in the sun.为阳光下的生活而做。

Los Angeles has a lot of sun. Most of the hats made for it are caps: hot on the head, heavy on the hair, and impossible to wear with a blazer.洛杉矶的太阳很大。为它准备的帽子大多是棒球帽：闷头、压头发，配上班的衣服也不好看。

We wanted something else. A wide brim that actually shades your face. A hat that breathes, so you forget you're wearing it. A shape quiet enough for the office and pretty enough for a wedding. And when you walk inside, a hat that folds into your bag instead of taking up your hands.我们想要不一样的帽子：真正能遮住脸的宽帽檐；透气，戴着像没戴一样；够低调，可以戴去上班，也够好看，可以戴去婚礼；进到室内，还能折起来放进包里，不用一直拿在手上。

The first hats were made for friends. They kept asking where to buy one. So we started a house.最早的几顶帽子是做给朋友的。她们戴上之后总有人问：在哪里买的？于是就有了这个品牌。

Why Théa为什么叫 Théa

In Greek myth, Theia is the Titan of light, the mother of the sun, and the goddess who gives gold its shine. Maison is French for house. Together: a house of light.在希腊神话里，Theia 是光之女神，太阳的母亲，也是让黄金闪耀的女神。Maison 在法语里是“家”。合在一起，就是“光之家”。

What we believe我们相信

  • Light, small, easy to pack.轻、小、好收纳。 A hat should go where you go.帽子应该跟着你去任何地方。
  • Simple enough for everything.简单，什么都能配。 Quiet colors that work with what's already in your closet.安静的颜色，和你衣柜里的衣服都能搭。
  • Elegant, never fussy.优雅，但不花哨。 Made to make you feel good, not to shout.让你戴着舒服、自信，而不是抢风头。
